How it works

Capture

A headless browser visits your URL and records every element's position, size, and computed styles.

Transform

Raw data is normalised into an intermediate representation — a clean tree of typed design nodes.

Render

A Figma plugin reads the IR and creates fully editable frames, text layers, and shapes on your canvas.
